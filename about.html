🚀 About JobSphere

Connecting Talent With Opportunity

JobSphere is an AI-enhanced job and career portal designed to help students, job seekers and companies connect more effectively.

What is JobSphere?

JobSphere is a modern career platform that brings job discovery, skill assessment and career guidance together in one place.

Our goal is to make the job search process smarter, simpler and more personalized for every user.
